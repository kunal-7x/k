# LinkedIn post — "one prompt = a carousel" (ready to post)

I used to spend 30-45 minutes building a single carousel.

Open Canva. Pick a template. Fight the layout. Rewrite the copy four times. Export every slide.

Last week I built one in about 30 seconds.

One prompt to an AI: *"Make me a 7-slide carousel on [topic] — one idea per slide, punchy, strong hook."* It wrote the copy and laid out every slide. Ready to ship.

Here's the shift that actually matters: I stopped **designing** and started **directing**.

I bring the idea and the judgment about what's good. The AI handles the production. That's the whole game with these tools right now — they don't replace your taste, they delete the busywork between your idea and the finished asset.

If you publish carousels and you're still doing it by hand, you're spending 30 minutes on something that now takes 30 seconds. The gap only widens from here.

What's the one task in your content workflow you'd hand off first? 👇
